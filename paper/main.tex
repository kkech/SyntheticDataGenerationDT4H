\begin{abstract}
Patient-level cardiology data are difficult to share, which slows method development
and reproducibility. We release synthetic versions of the DataTools4Heart (DT4H) Use
Case~1 heart-failure cohort (4{,}694 patients, 249 variables spanning demographics,
conditions, medications, laboratory values, vital signs, and five-year outcomes),
generated by six model families with and without differential privacy (DP;
$\eps \in \{1, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20\}$) across replicate seeds---27 released datasets in
total. Uniquely, every generator was trained on a 75\% split, with the remaining
1{,}174 patients held out to calibrate all evaluations: fidelity is read against the
sampling-noise floor between two real samples, utility is tested on unseen patients,
and privacy is assessed with a four-attack suite (membership inference, attribute
inference, singling-out, linkability) whose null is the holdout itself. All attacks
performed at chance level for every dataset (mean membership-inference AUC $= 0.500$),
with zero verbatim records and zero records closer to the training data than unseen
real patients. Fidelity, however, dissociates by capability: marginal-based DP
synthesis reproduces categorical distributions and Kaplan--Meier survival curves
indistinguishably from real unseen patients but fabricates cross-column associations
and violates row-level clinical logic, while neural non-DP synthesis shows the
opposite profile. We quantify this dissociation with a novel row-coherence audit (363
rules mined from the real data), and release the complete pipeline---every number,
figure, and document regenerates from one command---together with an automated
release gate that all published files passed. The missingness of clinical data, which
carries meaning, is preserved end-to-end by a sentinel encoding decoded back to null
in the released files.
\end{abstract}

\section{Background \& Summary}

Heart failure is a leading cause of hospitalization and mortality, and data-driven
research on heart-failure cohorts is constrained by legitimate privacy protections on
patient-level electronic health record (EHR) data. Synthetic data---artificial
records drawn from a generative model of the real cohort---has been proposed as a
release mechanism that preserves statistical utility without exposing individuals
\citep{chen2021synthetic, gonzales2023synthetic}. Yet published synthetic-data
releases often share three weaknesses: fidelity is reported without a reference for
how much two \emph{real} samples differ; privacy is asserted from the generation
mechanism rather than tested by attack; and evaluation stops at column distributions
without asking whether individual synthetic rows are clinically coherent patients.

This Data Descriptor releases synthetic versions of the DataTools4Heart (DT4H) Use
Case~1 (UC1) heart-failure cohort and addresses all three weaknesses by design:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A held-out real sample calibrates everything.} Generators were trained
on 3{,}520 patients; 1{,}174 patients were held out before any generation. Every
fidelity metric is read against the \emph{sampling-noise floor}---the same metric
computed between the training and holdout samples---every utility result is tested on
the holdout, and every privacy attack uses the holdout as its population of real
non-members.
\item \textbf{Privacy is attacked, not asserted.} Beyond distance-to-closest-record
analysis, we run the four standard adversarial evaluations---membership inference,
attribute inference, singling-out, and linkability \citep{giomi2023unified}---on all
27 released datasets.
\item \textbf{Evaluation extends to row-level clinical coherence.} We mine 363
logical rules from the real data (e.g., cause-specific death implies all-cause death;
CKD stage must match the patient's own eGFR value) and report each dataset's
violation rate against the real holdout's own rate---real clinical data violates its
own logic occasionally, and that is the fair bar.
\end{enumerate}

The release covers six generative model families---Gaussian copula, TVAE, and CTGAN
\citep{patki2016sdv, xu2019modeling} without formal privacy guarantees, and MST
\citep{mckenna2021winning}, AIM \citep{mckenna2022aim}, and DP-CTGAN
\citep{rosenblatt2020differentially} with $(\eps, \delta)$-differential privacy
\citep{dwork2014algorithmic}---across a privacy-budget sweep
$\eps \in \{1, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20\}$ and replicate seeds, for 27 released datasets from
31 planned runs (AIM exceeded its compute budget above $\eps = 5$; see Technical
Validation).

Beyond the data themselves, two findings characterize the current state of tabular
synthesis on real clinical data. First, capabilities dissociate: the marginal-based
DP method (MST) reproduces categorical distributions \emph{below} the sampling-noise
floor and produces five-year Kaplan--Meier curves statistically indistinguishable
from unseen real patients, yet fabricates thousands of cross-column associations and
fails a row-coherence release gate; the neural non-DP method (TVAE) shows the
near-opposite profile. No evaluated model delivers marginal fidelity, joint
structure, and row coherence simultaneously---under DP or otherwise. Second, privacy
and fidelity did not trade off in the attack results: the best-fitting models were
exactly as unattackable as the worst, empirically supporting the premise that
population-level learning does not require individual-level memorization.

All results in this paper regenerate from a single command in the released pipeline,
which also produced every figure, the per-column codebook, and the datasheet's
technical content \citep{gebru2021datasheets}.

\section{Methods}

\subsection{Source data}

The source cohort comprises 4{,}694 adult heart-failure patients extracted from the
EHR of a DT4H clinical partner site under the project's federated protocol, using the
standardized onFHIR/Feast feature-extraction toolchain and the UC1 feature-set
definition (528 declared variables). Data were delivered into the project's secure
research environment; all processing, including generator training, occurred inside
that environment, and only aggregate statistics, synthetic records, and reports leave
it. Each row is one patient (source identifiers were verified 100\% unique before
removal).

The extracted table spans demographics; encounter characteristics; 30 symptom
indicators; 108 medication and 78 condition indicators; laboratory values, vital
signs, echocardiography and electrocardiography measurements (first/last values
within the observation window); NYHA class; and five-year follow-up outcomes:
cause-specific death and rehospitalization flags in windows from 7 days to 5 years,
plus days-to-event columns.

\subsection{Preprocessing: no imputation, missingness as information}

Clinical missingness carries meaning: a null days-to-death means the patient did not
die within follow-up; a null laboratory value means the test was not ordered, itself
a clinical signal. The pipeline therefore never imputes. Instead:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Numeric nulls} are encoded as a per-column \emph{sentinel} placed below
the observed range (offset $= \max(0.25 \times \text{range}, 1.0)$), so generators
model missingness jointly with values; after generation, any value below the
column's observed minimum decodes back to null. The full encoding map (per-column
sentinel, observed minimum, and a structural/``not measured'' label) is released as
public metadata.
\item \textbf{Categorical and boolean nulls} become an explicit ``Missing'' category.
\item Deterministic feature engineering combines medication and condition variants
($108 \to 54$ and $78 \to 31$ indicators), maps NYHA LOINC codes to ordinal 1--4,
prefers first/last over redundant aggregate columns, and drops identifiers,
datetimes, and near-unique identifier-like columns.
\end{itemize}

Preprocessing is provably distribution-preserving: comparing raw and preprocessed
data column-by-column yields Kolmogorov--Smirnov (KS) statistic 0.0, total variation
distance (TVD) 0.0, and missingness-rate difference 0.0 on every retained column.

The preprocessed table ($4{,}694 \times 249$; 211 modelled columns after holding out
38 constants that are re-attached verbatim) was split 75/25 into training (3{,}520)
and holdout (1{,}174) sets with a seeded permutation; the split manifest (row
positions only) is released. \textbf{No generator, and no classifier trained during
evaluation, ever saw the holdout.}

\subsection{Design decisions and pitfalls, with their evidence}
\label{sec:design}

Several choices in this pipeline depart from common practice; each was made in
response to a concrete, observed failure, and we record them because the failures
are general.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Sentinel placement and the decode floor.} Missingness sentinels sit
below the observed range by $\max(0.25 \times \text{range}, 1.0)$. Early runs
decoded at the sentinel--minimum midpoint, and continuous generators smeared mass
across the gap, releasing impossible ``observed'' values (negative HDL cholesterol
and troponin). The decode floor is therefore the \emph{observed minimum}: any value
below it is, by construction, not a value that occurs and becomes null. The stated
cost---a synthetic value slightly below the real minimum intended as tail
extrapolation is folded into missingness---is visible in the copula's sparse-lab
columns and reported rather than hidden.
\item \textbf{Leakage is checked before decoding.} A memorized training row would
have its sentinels turned to nulls by decoding and could then slip past an
exact-match comparison against the sentinel-encoded training frame. The
verbatim-record check therefore runs in sentinel space, before any decoding; the
standalone release gate repeats it in the released representation against a decoded
training frame, so both spaces are covered.
\item \textbf{Dataframe dtype traps are treated as correctness bugs.} Three were
caught by tests and gates rather than by inspection: SQL \texttt{Decimal} columns
arriving as Python objects and silently routing numeric labs into categorical
handling; \texttt{NaN} and null being distinct in the columnar engine, so an early
imputation pass shipped 82 \texttt{NaN} cells while the null gate read zero (the
gate now counts both and refuses to write); and null-free boolean columns retaining
a boolean dtype that a downstream synthesizer would model as continuous. All
type normalization is now dtype-driven rather than metadata-driven.
\item \textbf{Metadata is not trusted for identifier removal.} A source identifier
declared as an ordinary nominal variable (100\% unique) was caught only by a
near-uniqueness safety net ($>90\%$-unique string columns are dropped), which now
runs regardless of declared types.
\item \textbf{Constant columns are held out of training.} The 38 constants carry no
signal, waste model capacity, and---for DP models---privacy budget; they are
re-attached verbatim after sampling and excluded from every fidelity aggregate,
because a copy is not a modelling success.
\item \textbf{Public-bounds DP accounting.} At 61 continuous columns, DP bound
discovery alone would have cost ${\approx}4.9$ of the privacy budget---more than an
entire $\eps = 1$ run---and at default settings it failed outright at full scale.
Since the per-column domains are already released in the sentinel encoding map,
they are declared public and passed as explicit bounds, spending zero budget on
preprocessing. This single decision is what makes the low-\eps{} end of the sweep
exist.
\item \textbf{Seed replication must be verified, not assumed.} Two runs of the same
SDV synthesizer with different seeds produced byte-identical outputs: the library
pins its sampling RNG to a fixed internal seed unless a random state is set
explicitly. Every reported seed-variance number in this paper post-dates an explicit
per-run random state and a test asserting that outputs differ across seeds and
reproduce within one. We suspect published seed-replication claims built on similar
stacks deserve the same check.
\item \textbf{The pipeline is the only writer, and reports never repair.} Step
outputs are wiped and regenerated on every rerun; the evaluation re-decodes each
released file on a copy and loudly flags any cell an up-to-date decoder would
change (a stale file is scored as-is, never silently fixed); per-run summaries are
rewritten after every model so an interrupted campaign keeps everything already
finished; and per-run timeouts are calibrated from measured runtimes (a 6-hour cap
chosen so that MST's legitimate 2.9-hour fits survive while a wedged fit cannot
consume a night). The release gate that later blocked one of our own candidate
files (Section~\ref{sec:gate}) is this philosophy applied at the boundary.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Generative models and the run plan}

Six synthesizers were trained on the training split only
(Table~\ref{tab:runplan}), under a 31-run plan: three seeds for each non-DP model; a
privacy-budget sweep $\eps \in \{1, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20\}$ for each DP model plus two
additional seeds at the headline $\eps = 15$; AIM ran on the 50 most outcome-relevant
columns (selection by mean absolute association with the declared outcome variables,
with the utility-evaluation targets, age, sex, and NYHA class force-included; the
ranked selection is released).

\paragraph{DP accounting.} Column domains are treated as public metadata---they are
released with the dataset in the sentinel encoding map---and passed to the DP
synthesizers as explicit bounds, so no privacy budget is spent on bound discovery and
the entire \eps{} applies to synthesis at every sweep point (this also makes
$\eps = 1$ feasible at 61 continuous columns, where DP bound discovery alone would
exceed the budget). $\delta = 10^{-9}$ (library default). AIM's measurement rounds
were capped at $3\times$ the column count (an accuracy/runtime hyperparameter that
leaves the $(\eps, \delta)$ guarantee unchanged); DP-CTGAN used the memory-stable
configuration (300 epochs, batch 50) established for the training GPU.

Each run is independently reproducible: per-run seed, library versions, git commit,
hardware, and the SHA-256 of the training file are recorded, and the SDV sampling RNG
is set explicitly per run (SDV otherwise pins sampling to a fixed internal seed,
which would silently void seed replication). Each fitted generator is retained inside
the secure environment (never released) so additional records, including
condition-filtered cohorts, can be produced without retraining. Every synthetic
dataset contains 3{,}520 records (matching the training size), was checked for
verbatim training-row reproduction \emph{before} sentinel decoding (zero found in
all runs), and was then decoded to the released representation in which nulls are
nulls.

The full generation campaign consumed 38.1 hours of wall time on a single
16\,GB NVIDIA T4 node (per-run costs in Appendix Table~\ref{tab:runs}: seconds for
the copula, 6/11 minutes for TVAE/CTGAN, ${\sim}33$ minutes per DP-CTGAN run, and
${\sim}2.9$ hours per MST run, which dominates); the analysis stages add
${\sim}2$ hours and rerun independently.

Of 31 planned runs, 27 completed. AIM at $\eps \geq 8$ exceeded a 2-hour per-run
compute cap even at 50 columns: AIM's measurement effort grows with the privacy
budget (low-\eps{} runs terminate early on budget exhaustion), which we report as a
practical scaling result for Private-PGM-based synthesis \citep{mckenna2019graphical}.

\subsection{Evaluation framework}

All evaluations run in the released (decoded) representation unless stated
otherwise, and every metric is calibrated by a real-vs-real reference:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Marginal fidelity.} Per-column two-sample KS (numeric, observed
values) and TVD (categorical, including the Missing category), plus the mean
absolute difference in numeric missingness rates; aggregated over the 211 modelled
columns (the 38 verbatim constants are excluded from aggregates as copies, not
modelling successes; full-schema aggregates are also reported). \emph{Floor:} train
vs holdout.
\item \textbf{Association structure.} Absolute change in Spearman correlation
(numeric--numeric), Cram\'er's V (categorical--categorical), and correlation ratio
(numeric--categorical) over ${\sim}24{,}000$ measurable pairs, plus a
\emph{fabricated-association} count: pairs with real $|$association$| < 0.1$ rendered
$> 0.5$ synthetically. \emph{Floor:} train-profile vs holdout-profile deltas.
\item \textbf{Full-joint distinguishability.} Classifier two-sample test
\citep[C2ST;][]{lopezpaz2017revisiting}: held-out AUC of a gradient-boosting
classifier separating real from synthetic rows. \emph{Floor:} the same test on train
vs holdout.
\item \textbf{Row-level clinical coherence.} 363 rules---351 boolean implications
mined from the training split (support $\geq 50$; $\leq 0.2\%$ training violations),
4 learned category-range consistencies (CKD stage vs eGFR; hyperkalemia grade vs
potassium), and 8 survival-logic checks (window flags vs recorded days-to-event;
follow-up bounds)---evaluated per row. \emph{Floor:} the holdout's own violation
rate.
\item \textbf{Survival fidelity.} For all-cause death and heart-failure
rehospitalization: a recorded days-to-event is an event; a null is administrative
censoring at 1{,}825 days. Kaplan--Meier curves; log-rank tests vs the holdout
(\emph{floor:} train vs holdout log-rank); and effect-estimate replication---the same
Cox proportional-hazards model (age, sex, NYHA, eGFR, sodium, systolic blood
pressure; standardized; complete-case) fitted on real and synthetic data
\citep{davidsonpilon2019lifelines}, compared by coefficient sign agreement and
magnitude error.
\item \textbf{Downstream utility (TSTR).} Train-synthetic-test-real
\citep{esteban2017realvalued}: classifiers trained on the real training split
(baseline) and on each synthetic dataset, all tested on the holdout, for five
family-diversified outcome targets including all-cause mortality; two model classes
(histogram gradient boosting; regularized logistic regression;
\citealp{pedregosa2011scikit}) and an augmentation arm (real + synthetic vs real
alone).
\item \textbf{Privacy.} (i) Distance to closest training record (Gower-style mixed
distance in sentinel space, so proximity requires matching values \emph{and}
missingness pattern), referenced to the holdout-to-train distance distribution---the
empirical profile of an innocent unseen patient; (ii) membership inference:
nearest-synthetic-record distance attack scored as AUC over training members vs
holdout non-members with bootstrap 95\% CIs; (iii) attribute inference from
quasi-identifiers (age, sex, admission year), reported as \emph{membership
advantage} (member minus non-member attack accuracy---population-level inference is
the purpose of releasing data; only member-specific advantage is leakage); (iv)
singling-out and linkability risks via Anonymeter \citep{giomi2023unified}.
\item \textbf{Subgroup fidelity.} All marginal aggregates recomputed within sex and
age strata, each against its own stratum noise floor.
\item \textbf{Release gate.} An automated go/no-go check per candidate file: schema
conformity, decode freshness, zero verbatim training rows, coherence within a stated
policy ($\leq \max(10\times$ the holdout's own violation rate, $+1$ percentage
point$)$), and a distance spot-check against the holdout threshold.
\end{itemize}

\section{Data Records}

The release comprises, per generative run, one CSV of 3{,}520 synthetic patient
records with the 249-column released schema (width-limited AIM runs: their 50-column
subset plus constants), named
\texttt{DT4H\_Synthetic\_<model>[\_eps<\eps>]\_seed<s>.csv}. Accompanying records:

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{DT4H\_Codebook.md}---per-column type, description (from the UC1
feature-set metadata), observed range or category list, missingness rate, and the
\emph{meaning} of a null for that column (structural ``no event'' vs ``not
measured'');
\item \texttt{DT4H\_Numeric\_Missing\_Encoding.json}---the public sentinel/bounds
map;
\item \texttt{DT4H\_Holdout\_Split.json}---the seeded split manifest (row positions
only);
\item the complete evaluation reports (fidelity, associations, coherence rule set
and audit, survival, utility, privacy assessment, privacy attacks), the generation
summary with full per-run provenance, eight figures (PNG and PDF), the datasheet
\citep{gebru2021datasheets}, the environment freeze (\texttt{pip freeze}), and
per-file release-gate reports.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The privacy-budget sweep}

Across $\eps = 1 \to 20$ (Figure~\ref{fig:eps}), MST improves monotonically (TVD
$0.024 \to 0.0014$; missingness MAD $0.032 \to 0.0019$) and saturates above
$\eps \approx 10$--$15$, empirically supporting $\eps = 15$ as the operating point:
$\eps = 20$ buys nothing measurable. DP-CTGAN is flat and poor across the entire
sweep (KS 0.73--0.90 at every \eps): its failure is adversarial-training
instability, not privacy noise---a budget-independent negative result for DP-GANs at
this width. AIM outperforms MST on its column subset at equal \eps{} (KS 0.326 vs
0.486 at $\eps = 1$) but could not complete above $\eps = 5$ within a 2-hour cap.

\subsection{Joint structure, fabricated associations, and full-joint
distinguishability}

The association noise floor is itself substantial at this sample size (mean
$|\Delta|$ 0.065 numeric--numeric, 0.022 categorical--categorical)---a caution for
the field against reporting association errors without it. TVAE preserves pairwise
structure best (mean $|\Delta|$ 0.086 numeric--numeric---near the floor---with
71\%/82\%/89\% of pairs within 0.1 across the three pair types;
Table~\ref{tab:assoc}). MST, despite near-perfect marginals, preserves only
${\sim}22$--$30\%$ of pairs and \textbf{fabricates thousands of strong associations
that do not exist in the real data} (real $|$association$| < 0.1$ rendered $> 0.5$;
2{,}576--3{,}547 pairs across its runs)---its association error is flat across the
entire \eps{} sweep, demonstrating a model-class ceiling that privacy budget cannot
buy back: MST measures only a spanning tree of pairwise marginals. CTGAN, notably,
fabricates none.

independent of, and in our results orthogonal to, privacy risk.

\subsection{Row-level clinical coherence}

Real unseen patients violate the 363 mined rules in 0.23\% of applicable checks
(training split: 0.014\%). Synthetic violation rates

\subsection{Survival fidelity and effect replication}

Five-year all-cause mortality is 40.2\% in the training split (1{,}421/3{,}520
events) and 41.0\% in the holdout (log-rank train-vs-holdout $p = 0.68$, the
sampling-noise calibration). \textbf{All MST runs at $\eps \geq 15$ produce
Kaplan--Meier curves statistically indistinguishable from the holdout for both
endpoints} (all-cause death and heart-failure rehospitalization; log-rank
$p > 0.05$); TVAE additionally passes for rehospitalization
(Figure~\ref{fig:km}). DP-CTGAN generates no deaths at all---its curve is flat at
1.0.

The Cox model on real data recovers the expected clinical epidemiology (standardized
log-hazard ratios: age $+0.29$, NYHA $+0.18$, male $+0.08$, eGFR $-0.21$, sodium
$-0.09$, systolic BP $-0.06$). Fitted on synthetic data
(Table~\ref{tab:survival}), the Gaussian copula and MST ($\eps{=}15$) reproduce 5/6
coefficient signs with mean absolute coefficient errors of 0.086 and 0.144
respectively; TVAE matches 5/6 signs but with an unstable magnitude on one
covariate. Research conclusions at the direction-of-effect level are largely
recoverable from the better synthetic datasets.

\subsection{Downstream utility}

Real-data baselines on the five outcome targets range AUC 0.54--0.69 (intrinsically
hard targets at this cohort size; the CV/non-CV rehospitalization-reason targets are
near-complements, so effectively three independent problems). TSTR gaps vs baseline

smaller gaps under the simpler model class indicate that part of the tree-model gap
reflects fine joint structure rather than unusable signal. Augmenting real training
data with synthetic records neither helps nor hurts (deltas $-0.03$ to

\subsection{Privacy: distances and the four-attack suite}

An unseen real patient's nearest-training-record distance has 5th percentile 0.063
and median 0.103 (Gower-style, in sentinel space). Across all 27 synthetic datasets:
zero exact matches; the closest any synthetic record comes to any training record is
0.414---four times the innocent-record median---and \textbf{0.0\% of synthetic
records fall below the holdout's 5th-percentile threshold in every run}
(${\sim}5\%$ would be expected even for a perfectly innocent generator;
Figure~\ref{fig:dcr}). Nearest-neighbor distance ratios $\approx 0.99$ show
synthetic records sitting between real records, not locked onto individuals.

The attack suite (Table~\ref{tab:privacy}; Figure~\ref{fig:mia}): mean
membership-inference AUC across the 27 datasets is 0.5000, with no bootstrap 95\% CI
excluding 0.5 (maximum single AUC 0.520, CI $[0.499, 0.538]$). Worst
attribute-inference membership advantage: $+3.6$ percentage points (near noise).
Singling-out risk: mean 0.006, max 0.036; linkability risk: mean 0.0006, max 0.010
(0--1 scales). Notably, attack performance is unrelated to fidelity rank---the
best-fitting models are exactly as unattackable as the worst---and the DP models
additionally carry their formal $(\eps, \delta)$ guarantee.

\section{Usage Notes}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Which file for which purpose.} For general modelling,
feature-importance work, and teaching: the TVAE datasets (gate-cleared; best joints
and utility). For categorical epidemiology, missingness-pattern research, and
survival-curve work under a formal privacy guarantee: MST $\eps = 15$---but
\emph{never} trust its cross-column correlations, and note it did not pass the
row-coherence gate. AIM $\eps = 5$ offers the most internally coherent rows over its
50-column subset. DP-CTGAN files are included for benchmark completeness only.
\item \textbf{Rows are population members, not case studies.} Even the best datasets
show ${\sim}10\times$ the real data's rate of logical inconsistency per row, and
whole rows are machine-distinguishable from real ones. Do not present individual
synthetic records as realistic patients.
\item \textbf{Nulls carry meaning}---consult the codebook before treating
missingness as noise; time-to-event nulls are administrative censoring, laboratory
nulls are ``not ordered.''
\item \textbf{Effect sizes attenuate.} Expect directionally correct but shrunken
associations and AUCs; confirmatory analyses require the real data under DT4H
governance.
\item Conditional cohorts (e.g., ``500 female patients over 80'') can be produced
from the retained generators inside the secure environment on request; outputs pass
the same release gate.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations}

Single clinical site and a modest cohort (4{,}694 patients): absolute
fidelity---particularly for laboratory values observed in few patients---is
sample-size-limited, and the pipeline is designed to rerun unchanged on the
forthcoming larger extract. AIM's privacy-budget sweep is incomplete above
$\eps = 5$ (a Private-PGM scaling result we report rather than hide). The C2ST
result conflates genuine joint imperfection with value-granularity artifacts; a
granularity-snapping decoder is future work, as is constraint-aware sampling using
the released rule set, which would address the residual row-coherence gap without
retraining. Cram\'er's V is reported without small-sample bias correction
(identically for real and synthetic, so comparisons are unaffected). The
membership-inference evaluation uses a distance attack; stronger learned attacks are
possible in principle, though the DP models' formal guarantee bounds all of them.

\section{Future Work}
% Internal roadmap version: trim or fold into Limitations for submission.

The evaluation itself localizes what to improve and how much each remedy costs. We
group the roadmap by what it requires of the release pipeline.

\subsection*{Bounded additions --- no full regeneration}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Rank/quantile transforms for heavy-tailed laboratory values},
applied model-side (inverted at decode) for the neural synthesizers. The fidelity
audit shows the numeric gap concentrated exactly there; this is the single largest
available fidelity lever, at ${\sim}2$ hours of GPU time (non-DP models only).
\item \textbf{Constraint-aware sampling.} The released 363-rule set already
functions as an executable specification of clinical coherence; a reject-or-repair
sampling layer over the retained generators would push every model's violation rate
toward the real 0.23\% without retraining---and would likely flip the MST release-gate
verdict. This is the remedy for the model-class component of the coherence gap;
more data addresses only the sample-size component.
\item \textbf{Granularity-snapping decode.} Rounding synthetic numerics to each
column's empirical granularity (integer ages and blood pressures, two-decimal
assays) removes the trivial component of C2ST separability and makes the residual
AUC a genuine joint-structure measure.
\item \textbf{Completing the AIM curve} at $\eps \geq 8$ via a tighter measurement-
rounds cap or a 40-column subset, resolving the ${\eps = 5}$ association anomaly and
finishing the strongest-DP-contender comparison.
\item \textbf{TVAE capacity sweep} (epochs, latent and compression widths $\times$
seeds): the release candidate currently runs at library defaults.
\item \textbf{A low-\eps{} anchor} ($\eps = 0.5$) to locate where marginal-based DP
actually breaks; the current sweep shows MST already usable at $\eps = 1$.
\item \textbf{Sentinel-design ablation}: missingness-indicator-plus-value encoding
vs the sentinel scheme, on TVAE, to quantify the design's contribution rather than
argue it.
\item \textbf{Learned membership-inference attacks} (shadow-model or
density-based) to complement the distance attack; the DP models' formal guarantee
bounds these by construction, but the non-DP release candidate deserves the
stronger adversary.
\item \textbf{A diffusion-model baseline} (TabDDPM-class), the current fidelity
frontier, dependency-permitting in the secure environment.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Full-regeneration campaign}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The larger multi-site extract.} Every sample-size-limited result
(sparse-lab fidelity, joint structure, utility baselines, coherence generalization)
improves with $n$; the frozen pipeline reruns the entire campaign---generation,
validation, figures, documentation, gating---from one command on the new cohort,
and the noise floors tighten automatically, making every test stricter as the data
grow.
\item \textbf{Transforms promoted into preprocessing} (affecting the DP models'
binning as well), conditional on the model-side transform (item~1 above) proving
its value.
\end{enumerate}
