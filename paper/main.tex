\begin{abstract}
Patient-level cardiology data are difficult to share, which slows method development
and reproducibility. We release synthetic versions of the DataTools4Heart (DT4H) Use
Case~1 heart-failure cohort (4{,}694 patients, 249 variables spanning demographics,
conditions, medications, laboratory values, vital signs, and five-year outcomes),
generated by nine model families with and without differential privacy (DP;
$\eps \in \{0.5, 1, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20\}$) across replicate seeds and design
variants---43 completed datasets from a 51-run plan. Uniquely, every generator was
trained on a 75\% split, with the remaining 1{,}174 patients held out to calibrate
all evaluations at matched sample sizes: fidelity is read against the sampling-noise
floor between two real samples scored at the same geometry, utility is tested on
unseen patients with bootstrap uncertainty, and privacy is assessed with a
five-attack suite (distance and learned membership inference, attribute inference,
singling-out, linkability) whose null is the holdout itself. All attacks performed
at or near chance for every dataset (worst membership-inference AUC 0.531), with
zero verbatim records in any file; the calibrated distance profile separates the
model families---TVAE places 10--13\% and MST 5--29\% of records nearer the
training data than the natural 5\% of real unseen patients, while every other
family places none. A corrected row-coherence audit (233 rules mined from the real
data with a decidable-denominator criterion) shows real unseen patients violate
clinical logic in 0.8\% of rows while the best synthetic dataset violates it in
21\%---capabilities dissociate: marginal-based DP synthesis reproduces categorical
distributions at the noise floor and passes equivalence tests on Kaplan--Meier
survival, yet fabricates thousands of cross-column associations, while neural
non-DP synthesis shows the near-opposite profile. An automated two-tier release
gate (open release; controlled access under a data-use agreement) clears no file
for open release and exactly one---a distance-filtered TVAE candidate---for
controlled access, a tiering we propose as the honest release standard for current
tabular synthesis. The complete pipeline, the a-priori public-domain declaration
that makes the DP accounting sound, and every report regenerate from one command;
clinical missingness is preserved end-to-end by a sentinel encoding decoded back to
null in the released files. All results are from the first clinical site of the
DT4H federation; the site-portable pipeline is being rolled out to the partner
sites, whose campaigns will reproduce every table and figure on their own cohorts
under identical, noise-floor-calibrated criteria.
\end{abstract}

\section{Background \& Summary}

Heart failure is a leading cause of hospitalization and mortality, and data-driven
research on heart-failure cohorts is constrained by legitimate privacy protections on
patient-level electronic health record (EHR) data. Synthetic data---artificial
records drawn from a generative model of the real cohort---has been proposed as a
release mechanism that preserves statistical utility without exposing individuals
\citep{chen2021synthetic, hernandez2022synthetic, gonzales2023synthetic}. Yet published synthetic-data
releases often share three weaknesses: fidelity is reported without a reference for
how much two \emph{real} samples differ; privacy is asserted from the generation
mechanism rather than tested by attack; and evaluation stops at column distributions
without asking whether individual synthetic rows are clinically coherent patients.

This Data Descriptor releases synthetic versions of the DataTools4Heart (DT4H) Use
Case~1 (UC1) heart-failure cohort and addresses all three weaknesses by design:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A held-out real sample calibrates everything---at matched sample
sizes.} Generators were trained on 3{,}520 patients; 1{,}174 patients were held out
before any generation. Every fidelity metric is read against the
\emph{sampling-noise floor}---the same metric computed between the training and
holdout samples---and, because two-sample statistics scale with sample size, every
synthetic comparison is scored at the floor's own $(n_\text{train},
n_\text{holdout})$ geometry, so floor and synthetic numbers are exchangeable.
Every utility result is tested on the holdout with bootstrap uncertainty, and every
privacy attack uses the holdout as its population of real non-members.
\item \textbf{Privacy is attacked, not asserted.} Empirical attacks are the
standard synthetic-data claims are increasingly held to
\citep{stadler2022synthetic}: beyond distance-to-closest-record analysis, we run
five adversarial evaluations---a distance and a cross-validated learned membership
inference attack \citep{shokri2017membership}, attribute inference, singling-out,
and linkability \citep{giomi2023unified}---on all 43 completed datasets, plus an
attack-derived empirical lower bound on the effective privacy loss.
\item \textbf{Evaluation extends to row-level clinical coherence.} We mine 233
logical rules from the real data (e.g., cause-specific death implies all-cause
death; CKD stage must match the patient's own eGFR value), with rule selection and
rule evaluation sharing one decidable-rows denominator, and report each dataset's
violation rate---and the share of \emph{rows} carrying any violation---against the
real holdout's own values. Real unseen patients violate the rule set in 0.024\% of
checks (0.8\% of rows); that, not zero, is the fair bar, and it is roughly ten
times stricter than the bar an uncorrected mining denominator produces.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Contributions.} Beyond the datasets, this work contributes, to our
knowledge for the first time in a clinical synthetic-data release: (i) a
\emph{matched-sample-size noise-floor calibration}---every fidelity, subgroup,
association, and distinguishability statistic scored at the real-vs-real
reference's own geometry, with the measured consequence that a mismatched floor
had overstated the flagship fidelity margin by a factor of five; (ii) a
\emph{row-coherence audit} with a consistent decidable-rows denominator, a
per-row violation share, and an explicit evasion check (the Missing-consequent
share), which together reveal that real clinical data violates its own logic an
order of magnitude less often than previously measured; (iii) an
\emph{a-priori public-domain declaration workflow} for DP synthesis---per-column
ranges justified from clinical knowledge, assay limits, ordinal scales, or study
design, reviewed before fitting and enforced by the pipeline at fit
time---closing the circular ``published-therefore-public'' bounds accounting we
show to be formally void; (iv) a \emph{two-tier release gate} (open release vs
data-use-agreement access) whose reports state the verdict under every policy,
whose distance criterion never relaxes, and whose verdicts rest on full-sample
measurements; (v) a five-attack privacy suite extended with an attack-derived
empirical privacy-loss bound and a density-controlled \emph{who-is-at-risk}
profile locating residual leakage within patient-atypicality strata; and (vi)
end-to-end preservation of clinical missingness via sentinel encoding. Each
methodological correction in (i)--(iv) is reported \emph{with its measured
effect on our own headline results}---the corrections tightened, weakened, or
overturned claims we had previously believed, and we regard that audit trail as
a contribution in itself.

The release covers nine generative model families: Gaussian copula, TVAE, and CTGAN
\citep{patki2016sdv, xu2019modeling} without formal privacy guarantees, plus
design variants of the neural models (a rank/quantile numeric transform, a
capacity- and epoch-scaled TVAE, and a missingness-indicator encoding ablation)
and an in-house Gaussian-DDPM tabular diffusion baseline; and MST
\citep{mckenna2021winning}, AIM \citep{mckenna2022aim}, DP-CTGAN
\citep{rosenblatt2020differentially}, and PATE-CTGAN with
$(\eps, \delta)$-differential privacy \citep{dwork2014algorithmic}---across a
privacy-budget sweep $\eps \in \{0.5, 1, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20\}$ and replicate seeds,
for 43 completed datasets from a 51-run plan (AIM exceeded its compute cap above
$\eps = 1$ in this campaign; see Technical Validation).

Beyond the data themselves, two findings characterize the current state of tabular
synthesis on real clinical data. First, capabilities dissociate: the marginal-based
DP method (MST) reproduces categorical distributions and missingness patterns at
the sampling-noise floor and passes formal equivalence tests on five-year
Kaplan--Meier survival, yet fabricates thousands of cross-column associations and
leaves a fifth of its rows carrying a clinical-logic violation; the neural non-DP
family (TVAE) shows the near-opposite profile. No evaluated model delivers marginal
fidelity, joint structure, and row coherence simultaneously---under DP or
otherwise---and against the corrected real-data bar (0.8\% of real rows carrying a
violation), none comes within an order of magnitude on row logic. Second, the
privacy--fidelity trade-off is real but small and concentrated exactly where theory
predicts: the TVAE family is the only one whose records sit measurably closer to
the training data than real unseen patients do (10--13\% below the unseen-patient
p5 distance threshold across all seven variants) and the only family with a
consistent, still-marginal membership-inference signal (distance-attack AUC
0.52--0.53, eight of nine full-width runs' CIs excluding 0.5, localized to
moderately atypical patients)---while the cross-validated \emph{learned} attack
stays at chance for every dataset and the attack-derived empirical privacy-loss
bound never exceeds $\hat\eps = 0.24$. This is empirical support both for
population-level learning without individual-level memorization and for what a
formal guarantee buys.

All results in this paper regenerate from a single command in the released pipeline,
which also produced every figure, the per-column codebook, and the datasheet's
technical content \citep{gebru2021datasheets}.

\section{Methods}

\subsection{Source data}

The source cohort comprises 4{,}694 adult heart-failure patients extracted from
the EHR of the first participating clinical site of the DT4H federation, under the
project's federated protocol, using the
standardized onFHIR/Feast feature-extraction toolchain and the UC1 feature-set
definition (528 declared variables). Data were delivered into the project's secure
research environment; all processing, including generator training, occurred inside
that environment, and only aggregate statistics, synthetic records, and reports leave
it. Each row is one patient (source identifiers were verified 100\% unique before
removal).

The extracted table spans demographics; encounter characteristics; 30 symptom
indicators; 108 medication and 78 condition indicators; laboratory values, vital
signs, echocardiography and electrocardiography measurements (first/last values
within the observation window); NYHA class; and five-year follow-up outcomes:
cause-specific death and rehospitalization flags in windows from 7 days to 5 years,
plus days-to-event columns.

\subsection{Preprocessing: no imputation, missingness as information}

Clinical missingness carries meaning: a null days-to-death means the patient did not
die within follow-up; a null laboratory value means the test was not ordered, itself
a clinical signal. The pipeline therefore never imputes. Instead:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Numeric nulls} are encoded as a per-column \emph{sentinel} placed below
the observed range (offset $= \max(0.25 \times \text{range}, 1.0)$), so generators
model missingness jointly with values; after generation, any value below the
column's observed minimum decodes back to null. The full encoding map (per-column
sentinel, observed minimum, and a structural/``not measured'' label) is released as
public metadata.
\item \textbf{Categorical and boolean nulls} become an explicit ``Missing'' category.
\item Deterministic feature engineering combines medication and condition variants
($108 \to 54$ and $78 \to 31$ indicators), maps NYHA LOINC codes to ordinal 1--4,
prefers first/last over redundant aggregate columns, and drops identifiers,
datetimes, and near-unique identifier-like columns.
\end{itemize}

Preprocessing is provably distribution-preserving: comparing raw and preprocessed
data column-by-column yields Kolmogorov--Smirnov (KS) statistic 0.0, total variation
distance (TVD) 0.0, and missingness-rate difference 0.0 on every retained column.

The preprocessed table ($4{,}694 \times 249$; 211 modelled columns after holding out
38 constants that are re-attached verbatim) was split 75/25 into training (3{,}520)
and holdout (1{,}174) sets with a seeded permutation; the split manifest (row
positions only) is released. \textbf{No generator, and no classifier trained during
evaluation, ever saw the holdout.}

\subsection{Design decisions and pitfalls, with their evidence}
\label{sec:design}

Several choices in this pipeline depart from common practice; each was made in
response to a concrete, observed failure, and we record them because the failures
are general.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Sentinel placement and the decode floor.} Missingness sentinels sit
below the observed range by $\max(0.25 \times \text{range}, 1.0)$. Early runs
decoded at the sentinel--minimum midpoint, and continuous generators smeared mass
across the gap, releasing impossible ``observed'' values (negative HDL cholesterol
and troponin). The decode floor is therefore the \emph{observed minimum}: any value
below it is, by construction, not a value that occurs and becomes null. The stated
cost---a synthetic value slightly below the real minimum intended as tail
extrapolation is folded into missingness---is visible in the copula's sparse-lab
columns and reported rather than hidden.
\item \textbf{Leakage is checked before decoding.} A memorized training row would
have its sentinels turned to nulls by decoding and could then slip past an
exact-match comparison against the sentinel-encoded training frame. The
verbatim-record check therefore runs in sentinel space, before any decoding; the
standalone release gate repeats it in the released representation against a decoded
training frame, so both spaces are covered.
\item \textbf{Dataframe dtype traps are treated as correctness bugs.} Three were
caught by tests and gates rather than by inspection: SQL \texttt{Decimal} columns
arriving as Python objects and silently routing numeric labs into categorical
handling; \texttt{NaN} and null being distinct in the columnar engine, so an early
imputation pass shipped 82 \texttt{NaN} cells while the null gate read zero (the
gate now counts both and refuses to write); and null-free boolean columns retaining
a boolean dtype that a downstream synthesizer would model as continuous. All
type normalization is now dtype-driven rather than metadata-driven.
\item \textbf{Metadata is not trusted for identifier removal.} A source identifier
declared as an ordinary nominal variable (100\% unique) was caught only by a
near-uniqueness safety net ($>90\%$-unique string columns are dropped), which now
runs regardless of declared types.
\item \textbf{Constant columns are held out of training.} The 38 constants carry no
signal, waste model capacity, and---for DP models---privacy budget; they are
re-attached verbatim after sampling and excluded from every fidelity aggregate,
because a copy is not a modelling success.
\item \textbf{``Public'' bounds must be public before looking, not published
after.} At 61 continuous columns, DP bound discovery alone would have cost
${\approx}4.9$ of the privacy budget---more than an entire $\eps = 1$ run---so
bounds must come from outside the budget. Our first accounting declared the
observed per-column extremes public \emph{because we released them}---a circular
argument that is formally void: the maximum of a column is one patient's value,
and a mechanism conditioned on it is not $\eps$-DP for any $\eps$ (remove that
patient and the mechanism changes). The corrected accounting replaces every bound
with an \emph{a-priori public domain}: a per-column range defensible from clinical
knowledge, assay reporting limits, ordinal scales, or study design alone
(released as \texttt{public\_domains.json}, each range carrying the basis it
rests on), reviewed by the authors before any fit. The pipeline enforces the
review (DP fits refuse to start without it) and aborts before training if any
training value escapes a declared range, so a too-narrow domain costs seconds,
not hours. Sentinel bounds are derived from the public domain by the same
public rule used for the data-side sentinels. This is what makes the low-\eps{}
end of the sweep exist \emph{and} keeps it sound; the residual data-dependent
choices---the categorical vocabularies fitted by the synthesis library and the
AIM column selection---are disclosed rather than hidden.
\item \textbf{Seed replication must be verified, not assumed.} Two runs of the same
SDV synthesizer with different seeds produced byte-identical outputs: the library
pins its sampling RNG to a fixed internal seed unless a random state is set
explicitly. Every reported seed-variance number in this paper post-dates an explicit
per-run random state and a test asserting that outputs differ across seeds and
reproduce within one. We suspect published seed-replication claims built on similar
stacks deserve the same check.
\item \textbf{The pipeline is the only writer, and reports never repair.} Step
outputs are wiped and regenerated on every rerun; the evaluation re-decodes each
released file on a copy and loudly flags any cell an up-to-date decoder would
change (a stale file is scored as-is, never silently fixed); per-run summaries are
rewritten after every model so an interrupted campaign keeps everything already
finished; and per-run timeouts are calibrated from measured runtimes (a 6-hour cap
chosen so that MST's legitimate 2.9-hour fits survive while a wedged fit cannot
consume a night). The release gate that blocks our own candidate
files (Section~\ref{sec:gate}) is this philosophy applied at the boundary.
\item \textbf{An impossible metric value is a bug report, not a result.} An early
campaign returned C2ST AUC $= 1.0$ with per-feature diagnostics showing
\emph{perfect} single-column separability on boolean columns shared by both
frames---mathematically impossible for genuinely shared categories. The cause was a
representation mismatch: generators emitted language-level booleans
(\texttt{True}) where the source data spelled lowercase strings (\texttt{true}),
rendering ${\sim}150$ categorical columns disjoint on disk. The artifact biased
fidelity metrics pessimistically but privacy metrics \emph{optimistically}:
cross-representation distances carried a constant mismatch term, and the corrected
rerun moved TSTR toward parity (TVAE logistic-regression gap $0.018 \to 0.007$)
while shrinking distance margins (TVAE share below the unseen-patient p5
threshold: $0\% \to 12\%$), flipping one gate verdict from pass to fail. The fix
is structural: categorical spellings are aligned to the source schema at
generation time and at every read site, and a representation audit (zero
cross-representation category mismatches) is now a mandatory release-gate check.
Every number in this paper post-dates that audit. We suspect this failure mode is
widespread wherever synthetic CSVs are compared against source extracts by
independent code paths.
\item \textbf{A noise floor is only a floor at the same sample size.} Our floor is
train vs holdout ($3{,}520$ vs $1{,}174$); our synthetic comparisons were
originally $3{,}520$ vs $3{,}520$. The expected two-sample KS under identity
scales as $\sqrt{1/n_a + 1/n_b}$, so the floor was inflated ${\sim}40\%$ relative
to what an ideal generator could score---``below the floor'' was too easy to
claim. Every synthetic frame is now subsampled to the holdout's row count
(averaged over seeded draws) before scoring, making floor and synthetic numbers
exchangeable. The correction cut MST's celebrated below-floor TVD margin from
$5.6\times$ to $1.14\times$: the claim survives, honestly. We suspect most
published ``real-vs-real reference'' comparisons carry this bias.
\item \textbf{Rule mining and rule evaluation must share a denominator.} The
coherence rules were originally selected using all antecedent-true rows as the
denominator while the audit counted only decidable rows (consequent not
Missing)---biasing selection toward rules with often-missing consequents and
making the committed training rates irreproducible by the auditor. Unifying on
decidable rows shrank the rule set from 363 to 233 and collapsed the real
holdout's violation rate from 0.23\% to 0.024\%: most of what we had been calling
``real data's own inconsistency'' was our denominator bug. Every coherence bar in
this paper is read against the corrected, ten-times-stricter baseline. The audit
also now reports the Missing-consequent share per frame, closing the evasion
channel where a generator drives its violation rate to zero by emitting
undecidable rows.
\item \textbf{A spot check near a threshold is a coin flip.} The release gate's
distance criterion originally used a 500-record sample; at the 10\% policy limit
that carries ${\sim}1.3$-point sampling error, and it flipped one borderline
verdict (a file measuring 10.1\% on all rows sampled at 8.2\% and ``passed'').
The gate now uses the privacy step's full-sample measurement wherever one exists
and names the measurement source in every report; spot checks remain only for
files with no full measurement.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Generative models and the run plan}

Nine synthesizer families were trained on the training split only
(Table~\ref{tab:runplan}), under a 51-run plan: three seeds for each core non-DP
model; single-seed design variants of the neural models (rank/quantile numeric
transform for TVAE and CTGAN, a capacity- and an epoch-scaled TVAE, a
missingness-indicator encoding ablation, and an in-house Gaussian-DDPM diffusion
baseline with an optional logic-guided sampler); a privacy-budget sweep
$\eps \in \{1, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20\}$ for MST, AIM, and DP-CTGAN plus two additional
seeds at the headline $\eps = 15$, an $\eps = 0.5$ anchor for MST, and PATE-CTGAN
at $\eps \in \{1, 5, 15\}$; AIM ran on the 50 (and, in a width ablation, 40) most
outcome-relevant columns (selection by mean absolute association with the declared
outcome variables, with the utility-evaluation targets, age, sex, and NYHA class
force-included; the ranked selection is released as ranks only---the raw
association scores are exact statistics of the training data and are not
published).

\paragraph{DP accounting.} Column domains are fixed \emph{a priori} in a reviewed
public declaration (\texttt{public\_domains.json}, released with the data): one
range per numeric column, each carrying the public basis it rests on---clinical
plausibility of recorded values, assay reporting limits, derivation caps, ordinal
scales, or the study's five-year follow-up window. The DP synthesizers receive
exactly these bounds (with sentinel bounds derived from the declared range by the
same public rule as the data-side sentinels), so no privacy budget is spent on
bound discovery and the entire \eps{} applies to synthesis at every sweep point;
this is also what makes $\eps \leq 1$ feasible at 61 continuous columns, where DP
bound discovery alone would exceed the budget. Every DP run records its
$(\eps, \delta)$ and the SHA-256 of the domain declaration it was bounded by;
$\delta$ is passed explicitly and reported per run. Two residual data-dependent
elements are disclosed rather than hidden: categorical vocabularies are fitted from
the private data by the synthesis library, and the AIM column selection is computed
on the training split. AIM's measurement rounds were capped at $3\times$ the column
count (an accuracy/runtime hyperparameter that leaves the $(\eps, \delta)$
guarantee unchanged); DP-CTGAN used the memory-stable configuration (300 epochs,
batch 50) established for the training GPU.

Each run is independently reproducible: per-run seed, library versions, git commit,
hardware, and the SHA-256 of the training file are recorded, and the SDV sampling RNG
is set explicitly per run (SDV otherwise pins sampling to a fixed internal seed,
which would silently void seed replication). Each fitted generator is retained inside
the secure environment (never released) so additional records, including
condition-filtered cohorts, can be produced without retraining. Every synthetic
dataset contains 3{,}520 records (matching the training size), was checked for
verbatim training-row reproduction \emph{before} sentinel decoding (zero found in
all runs), and was then decoded to the released representation in which nulls are
nulls.

The full generation campaign consumed ${\sim}54$ hours of wall time on a single
16\,GB NVIDIA T4 node (per-run costs in Appendix Table~\ref{tab:runs}: seconds for
the copula and the in-house DDPM, 6--13 minutes per TVAE/CTGAN fit, ${\sim}33$
minutes per DP-CTGAN run, up to 3.3 hours for PATE-CTGAN, and ${\sim}2.9$ hours
per MST run, which dominates); the analysis stages add a few hours and rerun
independently.

Of 51 planned runs, 43 completed. AIM at $\eps \geq 5$ exceeded its per-run
compute cap at both 50 and 40 columns in this campaign (it completed $\eps = 5$ in
1.5 hours in an earlier campaign under the same cap---the runtime is borderline,
and the cap has since been raised to 6 hours): AIM's measurement effort grows with
the privacy budget (low-\eps{} runs terminate early on budget exhaustion), which we
report as a practical scaling result for Private-PGM-based synthesis
\citep{mckenna2019graphical}.

\subsection{Evaluation framework}

All evaluations run in the released (decoded) representation unless stated
otherwise, and every metric is calibrated by a real-vs-real reference:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Marginal fidelity, at matched sample sizes.} Per-column two-sample
KS (numeric, observed values) and TVD (categorical, with nulls as an explicit
Missing category), plus the mean absolute difference in numeric missingness rates;
aggregated over the 211 modelled columns (the 38 verbatim constants are excluded
from aggregates as copies, not modelling successes). Because two-sample statistics
scale with $\sqrt{1/n_a + 1/n_b}$, every synthetic frame is subsampled to the
holdout's row count (mean over three seeded draws) before scoring, so every number
shares the floor's geometry; a numeric column rendered entirely missing on either
side scores KS $= 1$ rather than silently leaving the aggregate. \emph{Floor:}
train vs holdout.
\item \textbf{Association structure.} Absolute change in Spearman correlation
(numeric--numeric), Cram\'er's V
\citep[categorical--categorical;][]{cramer1946mathematical}, and correlation ratio
(numeric--categorical) over ${\sim}24{,}000$ measurable pairs, plus a
\emph{fabricated-association} count: pairs with real $|$association$| < 0.1$
rendered $> 0.5$ synthetically. Profiles are computed on the same holdout-sized
subsample as the marginals. \emph{Floor:} train-profile vs holdout-profile deltas.
\item \textbf{Full-joint distinguishability.} Classifier two-sample test
\citep[C2ST;][]{lopezpaz2017revisiting}: out-of-fold AUC (five-fold stratified
cross-validation) of a gradient-boosting classifier separating real from synthetic
rows, computed over the columns present in \emph{both} frames---width-limited runs
are scored on that intersection with their schema coverage reported separately,
never on schema width itself. \emph{Floor:} the same test on train vs holdout.
\item \textbf{Row-level clinical coherence.} 233 rules---221 boolean implications
mined from the training split (support and violation rate both computed on
\emph{decidable} rows: antecedent true and consequent not Missing; support
$\geq 50$; $\leq 0.2\%$ training violations), 4 learned category-range
consistencies (CKD stage vs eGFR; hyperkalemia grade vs potassium), and 8
survival-logic checks---evaluated per row. Three figures of merit per frame: the
per-check violation rate, the share of \emph{rows} carrying any violation (the
number a release decision turns on), and the Missing-consequent share (the evasion
channel: a generator can suppress its violation rate by emitting undecidable
rows). \emph{Floor:} the holdout's own values.
\item \textbf{Survival fidelity.} For all-cause death and heart-failure
rehospitalization: a recorded days-to-event is an event; a null is administrative
censoring at 1{,}825 days; a recorded time beyond the horizon is censored at it.
Kaplan--Meier curves \citep{kaplan1958nonparametric}; log-rank tests
\citep{mantel1966evaluation} vs the holdout (\emph{floor:} train vs holdout
log-rank); \emph{equivalence} testing (TOST: the 90\% CI of the survival
difference within $\pm 5$ percentage points at 1, 3, and 5 years---a positive
claim, unlike a non-significant log-rank), reported per seed; and effect-estimate
replication---the same Cox proportional-hazards model \citep{cox1972regression}
(age, sex, NYHA, eGFR, sodium, systolic blood pressure; complete-case, with
missing sex dropped rather than imputed) fitted on real and synthetic data
\citep{davidsonpilon2019lifelines}, every frame standardized by the \emph{real
training split's} covariate moments so scale infidelity cannot masquerade as
effect fidelity, compared by coefficient sign agreement and magnitude error over
the covariates present in both fits.
\item \textbf{Downstream utility (TSTR).} Train-synthetic-test-real
\citep{esteban2017realvalued}: classifiers trained on the real training split
(baseline) and on each synthetic dataset, all tested on the holdout, for five
family-diversified outcome targets including all-cause mortality; two model
classes (histogram gradient boosting; regularized logistic regression;
\citealp{pedregosa2011scikit}), Brier-score calibration, per-target bootstrap 95\%
CIs, and an augmentation arm with a size-matched control (real + synthetic is
compared against real + bootstrap-resampled real of the same size, so ``synthetic
adds signal'' is not confounded with ``more rows'').
\item \textbf{Privacy.} (i) Distance to closest training record
\citep[Gower-style mixed distance;][]{gower1971general} in sentinel space, so
proximity requires matching values \emph{and} missingness pattern, referenced to
the holdout-to-train distance distribution---the empirical profile of an innocent
unseen patient; (ii) membership inference \citep{shokri2017membership}: a
nearest-synthetic-record distance attack \emph{and} a cross-validated learned
attack (logistic regression over the nearest, second-nearest, and ratio distance
features, scored out-of-fold), both as AUC over training members vs holdout
non-members with bootstrap 95\% CIs, with the verdict taken on the \emph{worst}
attack's CI upper bound; a who-is-at-risk profile (within-stratum AUC across
member-atypicality quartiles, with non-members binned by the same cut points, so
population density cannot masquerade as leakage); and an attack-derived empirical
lower bound $\hat\eps$ on the effective privacy loss
(Clopper--Pearson-bounded TPR/FPR ratios); (iii) attribute inference from
quasi-identifiers (age, sex, admission year) under one shared encoder fitted on
the training split, reported as \emph{membership advantage} against the real
holdout's majority-class baseline; (iv) singling-out and linkability risks via
Anonymeter \citep{giomi2023unified}, with all frames compared in the released
(decoded) representation.
\item \textbf{Subgroup fidelity.} All marginal aggregates recomputed within sex
and age strata, each against its own stratum noise floor at matched stratum sizes.
\item \textbf{Release gate.} An automated go/no-go check per candidate file under
\emph{named, versioned policies}---\texttt{release} (open distribution) and
\texttt{controlled} (data-use-agreement access)---each report stating the verdict
under every policy: schema conformity, representation audit, decode freshness,
zero verbatim training rows, coherence within the policy
($\leq \max(10\times$ the holdout's own violation rate, $+1$ percentage point$)$
for \texttt{release}; $\max(20\times, +3)$ for \texttt{controlled}), and a
calibrated distance criterion identical under both policies (share of records
below the unseen-patient p5 threshold $\leq 2\times$ the natural 5\%; the privacy
margin is deliberately never relaxed). Distance verdicts use the privacy step's
full-sample measurement wherever it exists; width-limited candidates are measured
on the shared column subset against a p5 recomputed on that subset, with a
degenerate subset threshold failing rather than passing vacuously.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The privacy-budget sweep}

Across $\eps = 0.5 \to 20$ (Figure~\ref{fig:eps}), MST improves monotonically (TVD
$0.049 \to 0.009$; missingness MAD $0.044 \to 0.003$) and saturates above
$\eps \approx 8$--$15$, empirically supporting $\eps = 15$ as the operating point:
$\eps = 20$ buys nothing measurable, and the new $\eps = 0.5$ anchor locates where
marginal-based DP degrades appreciably while remaining far from collapse. DP-CTGAN
is flat and poor across the entire sweep (KS 0.89--0.96 at every \eps{} under the
failed-column accounting): its failure is adversarial-training instability, not
privacy noise---a budget-independent negative result for DP-GANs at this width.

(KS $0.57 \to 0.38$ from $\eps = 1$ to 15), trading MST's marginal precision for
markedly better row behavior (below). AIM outperforms MST on its column subset at
equal \eps{} (KS 0.326 vs 0.467 at $\eps = 1$) but exceeded its compute cap above
$\eps = 1$ in this campaign at both 50 and 40 columns.

\subsection{Row-level clinical coherence}

itself: the unseen holdout violates the 233 rules in 0.024\% of applicable checks,

\subsection{Survival fidelity and effect replication}

\subsection{Downstream utility}

Real-data baselines on the five outcome targets range AUC 0.54--0.69, with
per-target bootstrap 95\% CIs wide enough to enforce humility (e.g., the
mortality target's baseline CI spans 0.40--0.68 at 417 holdout positives): these
are intrinsically hard targets at this cohort size, and the CV/non-CV
rehospitalization-reason targets are near-complements, so effectively three
independent problems. TSTR gaps vs baseline (Figure~\ref{fig:tstr}): TVAE
$+0.049 \pm 0.028$ (gradient boosting) and $+0.007$ (logistic
regression---parity), with the epoch-scaled variant reaching $-0.004$ (nominally
above baseline) under the simpler learner; MST $\eps{=}15$: $+0.099 \pm 0.039$
and $+0.026$. The much smaller gaps under logistic regression indicate that most
of the tree-model gap reflects fine joint structure rather than unusable signal:
models of the complexity clinicians actually deploy at this cohort size transfer
near par. Brier-score calibration tells the same story (TVAE-family calibration
gaps 0.026--0.045). The augmentation arm is now controlled for size: against a
real-plus-bootstrapped-real control of identical training size, adding synthetic
records changes AUC by $-0.03$ to $+0.01$ across every group---synthetic data
substitutes; it does not add.

\subsection{Privacy: distances and the five-attack suite}

An unseen real patient's nearest-training-record distance has 5th percentile 0.063
and median 0.103 (Gower-style, in sentinel space); by construction, 5\% of real
unseen patients fall below the p5 threshold, which is therefore the calibrated
yardstick, not zero. Across all 43 synthetic datasets there are zero exact
matches, and the closest any synthetic record comes to a training record is 0.017
(TVAE)---slightly \emph{inside} the closest real unseen patient's 0.022, but of
the same magnitude, not a memorization signature. The bulk of the distance
distributions separates the model families (Figure~\ref{fig:dcr}): CTGAN, the
Gaussian copula, DDPM, DP-CTGAN, and AIM place \textbf{no} records below the p5
threshold and PATE-CTGAN at most 1.3\%, while the two closest-fitting families
concentrate mass nearer the training data than a fresh real sample
would---the TVAE family places 10--13\% of its records below the threshold across
all seven variants, and MST 14--29\% at $\eps \geq 1$ (falling to 4.7\% at the
$\eps = 0.5$ anchor: the one place the sweep buys distance margin). All shares
are full-sample measurements over every record.

The attack suite (Table~\ref{tab:privacy}; Figure~\ref{fig:mia}) reads the same
picture at the individual level, now with two attacks and a leakage profile. The
distance attack averages AUC 0.509 over the 43 datasets; the CIs excluding 0.5
belong exclusively to the TVAE family---eight of its nine full-width runs, worst
AUC 0.531 (CI $[0.512, 0.548]$)---a consistent, still-marginal echo of the
distance concentration, far below actionable individual inference, while every DP
model attacks at chance. The cross-validated \emph{learned} attack (nearest,
second-nearest, and ratio features, scored out-of-fold) stays at chance for every
dataset (mean 0.494; worst 0.523), and the attack-derived empirical privacy-loss
bound never exceeds $\hat\eps = 0.24$. The who-is-at-risk profile is flat at 0.5
in every atypicality stratum for MST and AIM, while the TVAE family shows a mild,
consistent elevation (${\sim}0.55$) concentrated in the third quartile: its
residual leakage sits with \emph{moderately} atypical patients, not the extreme
outliers. Worst attribute-inference membership advantage under the shared-encoder
attack: $+5.0$ percentage points (small, though above the earlier
mis-encoded estimate of $+3.6$). Singling-out risk: mean 0.006, max 0.053;
linkability risk: mean 0.0001, max 0.005 (0--1 scales, decoded representation).
The fidelity--privacy relationship thus lands exactly where theory puts it: the
best-fitting non-DP family carries the only measurable (and still marginal)
membership signal, and the DP models' chance-level attack results corroborate
what their guarantee will formally assert once re-fitted under the reviewed
domains. Notably, MST fails the distance criterion while attacking at exact
chance---a clean demonstration that record-proximity and attack-based privacy
measure different risks, and a release decision should consult both.

\subsection{Release gating}
\label{sec:gate}

The automated gate runs six mandatory checks---schema, representation, freshness,
verbatim leakage, row coherence, and distance---under two named policies whose
verdicts every report states side by side: \texttt{release} (open distribution;
coherence within $\max(10\times$ the holdout baseline, $+1$pp$) = 1.02\%$) and
\texttt{controlled} (data-use-agreement access; $\max(20\times, +3$pp$) =
3.02\%$). The distance criterion---at most twice the natural 5\% share below the
unseen-patient p5 threshold---is identical under both policies: the coherence
tolerance protects the \emph{recipient} and may be relaxed by contract; the
distance margin protects the \emph{patients} and is never relaxed.

Gating all 43 datasets against the corrected baselines yields the central
governance result of this release: \textbf{no file qualifies for open release,
and exactly one qualifies for controlled access}. Every generator exceeds the
open-release coherence bar (the nearest, TVAE-ind at 1.84\%, sits $75\times$
above the real holdout's rate); the entire TVAE family passes the controlled
coherence bar but fails distance (10--13\% below the threshold); MST fails both.
The one passing file is a \emph{post-processed candidate}: the TVAE-ind dataset
with its below-threshold distance tail removed by a disclosed nearest-record
rejection filter and re-sampled to size from the retained generator
(re-audited: coherence 1.95\%, 0\% of records below the threshold, zero verbatim
rows)---\texttt{release: FAIL / controlled: PASS}. All 44 gate reports (43 runs
plus the candidate) are released, including a verdict our own measurement
infrastructure overturned: a raw TVAE variant briefly ``passed'' controlled on a
distance spot check of 8.2\% whose full-sample value is 10.1\% (Methods,
design item 12).

We regard a gate that blocks every raw file its own authors produced, admits one
disclosed-filter derivative at the contract-bound tier, and prints both verdicts
on every report as measuring rather than rubber-stamping---and we propose this
two-tier outcome, uncomfortable as it is, as the honest release standard current
tabular synthesis actually supports on real clinical data.

\section{Usage Notes}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Which file for which purpose.} The only gate-cleared file---at the
\texttt{controlled} (data-use-agreement) tier---is the distance-filtered TVAE-ind
candidate: best joints and utility with the below-threshold distance tail removed
by a disclosed filter; use it for general modelling, feature-importance work, and
teaching under a DUA. The raw TVAE datasets carry the closest distance profile
(10--13\% of records nearer the training data than the unseen-patient p5
threshold, vs.\ a natural 5\%) and the only, though marginal,
membership-inference signal; treat them as internal benchmark material. For
categorical epidemiology, missingness-pattern research, and survival-curve work
under a (post-re-fit) formal privacy guarantee: MST $\eps = 15$---but \emph{never}
trust its cross-column correlations, and note its distance profile is the widest
of all (14--29\% below the threshold). Where the distance margin matters most,
prefer CTGAN, the copula, or PATE-CTGAN, which place ${\leq}1.3\%$ of records
below the threshold. PATE-CTGAN $\eps = 15$ is the balanced DP option: moderate
everything, near-zero fabrication, comfortable distances. DP-CTGAN and the
in-house DDPM files are included for benchmark completeness only.
\item \textbf{Rows are population members, not case studies.} Real unseen
patients carry a clinical-logic violation in 0.8\% of rows; the best synthetic
datasets in 21--31\%---a $25$--$40\times$ excess---and whole rows are
machine-distinguishable from real ones. Do not present individual synthetic
records as realistic patients.
\item \textbf{Nulls carry meaning}---consult the codebook before treating
missingness as noise; time-to-event nulls are administrative censoring, laboratory
nulls are ``not ordered.''
\item \textbf{Effect sizes attenuate.} Expect directionally correct but shrunken
associations and AUCs; confirmatory analyses require the real data under DT4H
governance.
\item Conditional cohorts (e.g., ``500 female patients over 80'') can be produced
from the retained generators inside the secure environment on request; outputs pass
the same release gate.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations}

Single clinical site and a modest cohort (4{,}694 patients): absolute
fidelity---particularly for laboratory values observed in few patients---is
sample-size-limited, and the pipeline is designed to rerun unchanged on the
forthcoming larger extract. The DP results reported here predate the
reviewed-public-domain re-fit; until that campaign replaces them, the DP
files carry chance-level empirical attack results but no
valid formal guarantee.
% REFIT: delete the two sentences above once the re-fit campaign lands. AIM's privacy-budget sweep is incomplete above $\eps = 1$
in this campaign (a Private-PGM scaling result we report rather than hide; the
raised compute cap gives $\eps = 5$ a realistic retry). The C2ST of
$\geq 0.997$ is measured to be genuine, distributed joint imperfection (the
granularity-snapping ablation leaves it unchanged), so closing it requires better
joint modelling, not decoding cosmetics; constraint-aware sampling using the
released rule set is the corresponding remedy for the row-coherence gap, whose
corrected real-data bar (0.8\% of rows) no current generator approaches. The
in-house diffusion baseline's poor showing reflects a diverged minimal
implementation (stabilized after this campaign), not evidence about diffusion
models as a class; a reference TabDDPM comparison remains future work. The
sentinel decode maps synthetic values below a column's observed minimum to null,
so a synthetic early event rendered below-range is read as censoring---an
asymmetry disclosed in the survival report and unrecoverable from the released
CSVs. Cram\'er's V is reported without small-sample bias correction (identically
for real and synthetic, so comparisons are unaffected). The membership-inference
suite now includes a cross-validated learned attack and an empirical
privacy-loss bound; shadow-model attacks with auxiliary data remain possible in
principle, and the DP models' formal guarantee will bound them only after the
re-fit.

\section{Future Work}

The evaluation itself localizes what to improve and how much each remedy costs.
Most of the previous roadmap's bounded items were executed in this revision and
their outcomes folded into the results above: the rank/quantile transform (TVAE's
best numeric fidelity, KS 0.184, joint structure unchanged); the granularity-snap
decode (confirmed cosmetic for C2ST, retained for face validity); the
nearest-record rejection filter (produced the one gate-cleared controlled-tier
candidate); the TVAE capacity and epoch sweep (marginal gains; the epoch-scaled
variant reaches TSTR parity under logistic regression); the $\eps = 0.5$ anchor
(marginal-based DP degrades gracefully, far from collapse); the sentinel-design
ablation (the indicator encoding matches the sentinel scheme's fidelity and gives
the best row coherence of the family---the design contributes robustness, not
magic); the learned membership attack and the empirical privacy-loss audit (both
at chance / $\hat\eps \leq 0.24$); and a first constraint-aware sampler
(logic-guided diffusion), inconclusive because its base model diverged. What
remains:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The DP re-fit campaign under the reviewed public domains}---the
condition for every formal $(\eps, \delta)$ claim in this paper, replacing
every DP-derived value reported here; and, within it, \textbf{completing the AIM curve} at
$\eps \geq 5$ under the raised compute cap.
\item \textbf{Constraint-aware sampling over a converged base model.} The
released 233-rule set is an executable specification of clinical coherence; a
reject-or-repair sampling layer over the retained generators is the remedy for
the model-class component of the coherence gap---the component the corrected
0.8\%-of-rows real bar shows no current generator approaches. More data
addresses only the sample-size component.
\item \textbf{A reference diffusion baseline} (TabDDPM-class),
dependency-permitting in the secure environment, replacing the diverged in-house
implementation before any claim about diffusion models is made.
\item \textbf{The federation rollout.} The results here are from one clinical
site; the frozen, site-portable pipeline (a partner repository, exported by an
allowlisted and verified procedure, runs the full campaign from one command
against any site's extract) is being deployed to the DT4H partner sites. Each
site's campaign reproduces every table, figure, gate report, and datasheet on
its own cohort under identical criteria; because all metrics are calibrated to
each site's own noise floor, results are comparable across sites by
construction, and every sample-size-limited result (sparse-lab fidelity, joint
structure, utility baselines, coherence generalization) tightens as cohorts
grow.
\item \textbf{Transforms promoted into preprocessing} (affecting the DP models'
binning as well), now that the model-side quantile transform has proven its
numeric-fidelity value.
\end{enumerate}
